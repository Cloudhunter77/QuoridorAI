Quoridor AI

Your turn — move your pawn or place a wall.

How to play
  • You are the blue pawn at the bottom. Reach the top row to win.
  • The AI is the red pawn at the top, racing for the bottom row.
  • On your turn either move one square (click a highlighted cell) or place a wall (click a slot between cells).
  • Walls are two cells long. Click a horizontal slot for a horizontal wall, a vertical slot for a vertical wall. A green preview means it's legal.
  • Walls block movement but can never fully seal off a player's path to their goal.
  • If pawns are face to face you may jump over the opponent.
